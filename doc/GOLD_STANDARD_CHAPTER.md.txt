# PhysicsRishi Gold Standard Chapter Specification
Version: 1.0
Status: Product Constitution
Owner: Founder + CTO

---

# 1. Product Philosophy

PhysicsRishi is NOT another teaching platform.

PhysicsRishi is an AI-powered Physics Revision & Assessment System built for serious JEE and NEET aspirants.

Our objective is NOT to teach Physics.

Our objective is to answer one question:

> "Am I really ready for the exam?"

Every chapter must help the student:

• Revise Faster
• Think Better
• Detect Weaknesses
• Build Confidence
• Improve Performance

The chapter should feel like a personal mentor rather than a digital textbook.

---

# 2. Golden Principles

Every chapter MUST satisfy these principles.

1. Maximum learning in minimum time.

2. No unnecessary theory.

3. Every screen must have one objective.

4. Every concept must increase exam performance.

5. Every question must teach thinking.

6. Reflection is more valuable than marks.

7. AI Mentor should discuss mistakes, not repeat theory.
