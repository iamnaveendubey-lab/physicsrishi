# PhysicsRishi Reflection Engine
Version: 1.0
Status: Core Product Architecture
Owner: Founder + CTO

---

# Vision

Reflection is NOT a marksheet.

Reflection is an AI-powered performance diagnosis system.

The purpose of Reflection is to answer one question:

> "What should this student do next to maximize exam performance?"

Marks are only an input.

The Reflection Engine produces a personalized action plan.

---

# Product Philosophy

Every coaching institute tells students

"You scored 72%."

PhysicsRishi tells students

• Why they scored 72%

• Which concepts caused the loss

• How much time is needed to fix them

• What should be studied next

• Whether they are actually exam-ready

Reflection is more valuable than marks.

# Mistake Classification

Every incorrect answer belongs to exactly one category.

Concept Error

Student does not know concept.

Application Error

Student knows concept but cannot apply.

Calculation Error

Arithmetic mistake.

Formula Error

Wrong formula selected.

Unit Error

Incorrect units.

Dimension Error

Dimensional mistake.

Reading Error

Misread question.

Time Pressure

Question skipped due to lack of time.

Guessing

Random answer.

These mistake categories become powerful analytics.
